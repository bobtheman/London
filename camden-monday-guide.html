Camden Town: A Monday Morning Guide

Monday 30 November 2026 · arriving Camden Town tube at 08:30 · updated 26 November 2026

Arrive at Camden Town tube at 08:30. The cafés and the canal are open from the start, but the markets don't open until 10:00. So start with breakfast and a walk along the canal, and do the markets after that.

  • Daylight: sunrise is around 07:40 and it gets dark by about 16:00.
  • Crowds: Monday morning is the quietest Camden gets.
  • Distance: everything here is within about 10 minutes' walk of the station, except Primrose Hill, which is about 15 minutes past Camden Lock.

Practical tips

  • Check hours the day before. The opening times here come from Google Maps listings checked on 26 November 2026, and cafés sometimes change them at short notice.
  • Dress for the cold. The towpath can be muddy, so wear decent shoes.
  • Bring a card. Most market stalls take card, and some bars, like The World's End, take nothing else.
  • Do the canal before 15:30. It's dark by about 16:00, and the canal is best seen in daylight.
